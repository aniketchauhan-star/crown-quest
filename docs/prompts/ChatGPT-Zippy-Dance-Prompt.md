# ChatGPT prompt: Zippy dances with his bag (sprite sheet)

One sprite sheet for the finale: Zippy stands on his feet, wearing the crown, a brown bag slung over his shoulder on his back, one hand holding the bag and the other raised to the sky, dancing and laughing.

## How to use it

1. Open ChatGPT and start a new chat (image generation must be available).
2. Attach the two pictures in `docs/prompts/ref/`:
   - `zippy reference.png`, so ChatGPT draws the same Zippy;
   - `crown.png`, so the crown on his head is exactly the game's crown.
3. Paste the prompt below and send it.
4. Download the image, save it as `zippy dance.png`, and give it to Claude.

## The prompt (copy everything in the box)

```
Create ONE sprite sheet for a cartoon children's math game: Zippy the monkey dances and laughs, wearing a golden crown, with a brown bag slung over his shoulder on his back.

Zippy must be EXACTLY the same character as in the attached picture (zippy reference.png): the same face, orange-brown fur, cream face, hands, feet and tummy, big round ears, long curly tail and proportions, with the same bold dark-brown outline and soft cel shading with glossy highlights. Do not redesign him. In that picture he hangs by his tail; here he STANDS on his own two feet.

The crown must be EXACTLY the attached crown (crown.png): shiny gold, five points each topped with a gold ball, three red oval gems on the front (the middle gem is the biggest) and a gold band along the bottom. Same shape, colours and gems in every frame. Do not add or change anything on it.

The bag: a plain brown cloth sack, like a burlap sack, round and full, tied shut at the top with a thin rope. It is a bit smaller than his body. Nothing pokes out of it, and it has no writing, patches or symbols. Same bag, same size and same colour in every frame.

How he holds things (the same in ALL 8 frames):
- The crown sits on top of his head, between his ears. It never falls off, never covers his eyes, and is about as wide as his head.
- The bag is slung over his left shoulder (on the viewer's right) and hangs down behind his back. His left hand grips the tied top of the bag just above his shoulder.
- His right arm (on the viewer's left) is raised high up to the sky, above his head and crown, celebrating. It never covers his face or the crown.
- Keep the bag on the same shoulder and the raised arm on the same side in every frame. Do not mirror or flip him.

Canvas and layout:
- Wide landscape image, 1536 × 1024, with a fully transparent background. If transparency is not possible, use one flat, solid, pure magenta (#FF00FF) background with no gradient or texture.
- 8 frames in a 4 × 2 grid: 4 equal columns and 2 equal rows. One Zippy per frame, centred in his cell, with at least 40 px of empty space around him, his raised hand and the bag. Nothing touches or crosses into the next cell.
- He stands on his feet. Draw an invisible floor line near the bottom of every cell, at the same height in every cell: in every frame at least one of his feet rests on that line.
- Zippy is the same size in all 8 frames, and the same size as in zippy reference.png: his head is the same size in every frame.
- He faces the viewer, turned slightly to the viewer's right.
- He is laughing in every frame: big open-mouthed laugh, happy eyes (open or squeezed shut with joy).
- No text, numbers, labels, frame borders, grid lines, motion lines, music notes, sparkles, stars or shadows. No ground, no vine, no background. No other objects.

The 8 frames, left to right, top row first. They are one dance that loops: frame 8 leads straight back into frame 1.
1. Step: weight on one foot, the other foot lifted and tapping, hips swung to one side; raised hand open; laughing.
2. Bounce: both feet on the floor, knees bent; raised hand a fist pumping the sky; laughing with eyes squeezed shut.
3. Step the other way: the other knee lifted, hips swung to the other side; raised hand waving; laughing.
4. Wiggle: both feet on the floor, body and tail swaying to one side; raised hand with one finger pointing up at the sky; head tilted back, laughing.
5. Kick: standing on one foot, the other leg kicking out to the side; raised hand open with fingers spread; big laugh.
6. Bounce: both feet on the floor, knees bent; raised hand a fist pumping the sky; eyes squeezed shut, laughing.
7. Shimmy: both feet on the floor, shoulders and hips wiggling, the bag swinging a little on his back; raised hand waving; laughing.
8. Settle: standing on both feet, a small lean, ready to start the dance again; raised hand open; laughing.

Before you finish, check: 8 separate frames in a 4 × 2 grid; the same Zippy at the same size in every frame; his feet on the same floor line in every frame; the crown on his head in every frame, exactly like crown.png and the same size; the brown bag over his left shoulder, held by his left hand, in every frame; his right arm raised to the sky in every frame; he is laughing in every frame; the background is transparent; there is no text and nothing else.
```

## If something goes wrong (follow-up messages for the same chat)

- **The crown looks different from crown.png:**
  > Make the crown exactly like the attached crown.png: five gold points with gold balls, three red oval gems with the biggest in the middle, and a gold band at the bottom. Keep everything else the same.
- **The crown falls off, floats or covers his eyes:**
  > In all 8 frames, put the crown right on top of his head, between his ears, with his eyes fully visible. Keep everything else the same.
- **The bag is in front of him, in the wrong hand, or changes side:**
  > In all 8 frames, the brown sack is slung over his left shoulder (viewer's right) and hangs behind his back, with his left hand gripping its tied top above his shoulder. Keep everything else the same.
- **His free hand is not up in the sky:**
  > In all 8 frames, his right arm (viewer's left) is raised high above his head to the sky. Keep the dance poses, the bag and the crown.
- **Things poke out of the bag, or it has writing or patches:**
  > Make the bag a plain brown cloth sack tied at the top with a rope, with nothing coming out of it and no writing or patches. Keep everything else the same.
- **He is not laughing in some frames:**
  > Make him laugh with a big open mouth in all 8 frames. Keep the poses.
- **He hangs from his tail instead of standing:**
  > He must stand on his own feet in every frame, not hang. His tail curls up behind him. Keep the poses, the bag and the crown.
- **Zippy looks different from the reference:**
  > Make Zippy look exactly like zippy reference.png (same face, ears, fur colour and outline). Keep everything else the same.
- **The background is not transparent:**
  > Make the background fully transparent, or one flat pure magenta (#FF00FF). Keep everything else the same.
- **Zippy changes size between frames, or his feet jump up and down:**
  > Draw Zippy at the same size in all 8 frames, with his feet on the same floor line in every frame. Keep the poses.
- **Frames touch, or there are sparkles, music notes, shadows or labels:**
  > Keep exactly 8 frames in a 4 × 2 grid with empty space between them. Remove all sparkles, music notes, shadows, text and anything else.

## What happens next

Claude cuts the sheet into 8 frames and lines them up on Zippy's feet, so he dances in place without sliding. The finale then shows only the jungle background and Zippy dancing with his bag, looping frames 1 to 8.
