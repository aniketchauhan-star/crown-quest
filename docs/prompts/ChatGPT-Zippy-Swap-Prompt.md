# ChatGPT prompt: Zippy's swap sprite sheet (empty hands)

One sprite sheet of Zippy picking things up and swapping them, with nothing in his hands. The game puts the real gold bar (with the right number for each level) and the crown into his hands.

## How to use it

1. Open ChatGPT and start a new chat (image generation must be available).
2. Attach these two images from this folder, so ChatGPT draws the same Zippy:
   - `hold crown.png`
   - `hold crown and up.png`

   Don't attach the crown or gold bar images, or ChatGPT may draw them in his hands.
3. Paste the prompt below and send it.
4. Download the image and save it in this folder as `zippy swap.png`, then tell Claude.

## The prompt (copy everything in the box)

```
Create ONE sprite sheet for a cartoon children's math game: Zippy the monkey picking things up and swapping them, with EMPTY hands. The game adds the objects (a gold brick and a crown) into his hands later, so draw NO objects at all.

Zippy must be EXACTLY the same character as in the attached images: the same face, orange-brown fur, cream face and tummy, big round ears, curly tail and proportions, with the same bold dark-brown outline and soft cel shading with glossy highlights. Do not redesign him.

Canvas and layout:
- Wide landscape image, 1536 × 1024, with a fully transparent background. If transparency is not possible, use one flat, solid, pure magenta (#FF00FF) background with no gradient or texture.
- 8 frames in a 4 × 2 grid: 4 equal columns and 2 equal rows. One Zippy per frame, centred in his cell, with at least 40 px of empty space around him. Nothing touches or crosses into the next cell.
- In every frame Zippy hangs by his tail from a vine above, in the same hanging pose as the attached images (do NOT draw the vine). His tail curls at the top centre of the cell, at the same height in every frame.
- Zippy is the same size in all 8 frames: his head is the same size in every frame. Leave room below him for his arms to reach down.
- He faces the viewer's right side.
- His hands are always EMPTY and clearly visible, never hidden behind his body. When he "holds" something, his fingers curl around an empty space about the size of a small gold brick, with nothing drawn inside.
- No objects anywhere: no gold brick, no crown, no coins. No text, numbers, labels, frame borders, grid lines, motion lines, sparkles or shadows. No vine, no balance, no background.

The 8 frames, left to right, top row first:
1. Take: he reaches forward and down with both hands open, ready to take something from below, with an eager smile.
2. Hold: both hands together in front of his chest, curled as if holding a gold brick (an empty gap between his hands), happy and proud.
3. Reach: he swings down to the right with both arms stretched down, his hands still curled together as if holding the brick, determined.
4. Swap: at the bottom of the cell, his hands are apart at the same height: one hand opens as if letting go of something, the other hand curls as if grabbing something.
5. Lift: one curled hand comes up towards his chest; the other hand is open and relaxed. Excited face.
6. Ooh: both curled hands together at his chest as if hugging a crown, eyes wide, mouth open in wonder.
7. Look: both arms held out towards the viewer, hands curled as if showing off a crown, with a big proud smile.
8. Yes: one curled hand held out, a thumbs-up with the other hand, winking with a big grin.

Before you finish, check: 8 separate frames in a 4 × 2 grid; the same Zippy at the same size in every frame; the tail curl at the same height in every frame; his hands are empty and clearly visible in every frame; the background is transparent; there are no objects and no text anywhere.
```

## If something goes wrong (follow-up messages for the same chat)

- **He is holding something:**
  > Redraw the same sheet with his hands completely empty: no brick, no crown, no objects at all. Keep the same hand shapes and poses.
- **His hands are hidden or hard to see:**
  > Make both of his hands clearly visible in every frame, never behind his body. Keep everything else the same.
- **Zippy looks different from the attached images:**
  > Make Zippy look exactly like the attached images (same face, ears, fur colour and outline). Keep everything else the same.
- **The background is not transparent:**
  > Make the background fully transparent, or one flat pure magenta (#FF00FF). Keep everything else the same.
- **Zippy changes size between frames, or his tail moves up and down:**
  > Draw Zippy at the same size in all 8 frames, with his tail curl at the same height in every frame. Keep the poses.
- **Frames touch, or there are extra items or labels:**
  > Keep exactly 8 frames in a 4 × 2 grid with empty space between them. Remove all text, labels and anything else.

## What happens next

Claude cuts the sheet into 8 frames, lines them up on the tip of Zippy's tail like his other sheets, and marks where his hands grip in each frame. In the game, the real gold bar and the crown then sit in his hands while he:
- takes the bar from the child (frames 1–2);
- swings to the balance and swaps it for the crown (frames 3–5);
- shows the crown off (frames 6–8).
