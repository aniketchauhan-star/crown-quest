# ChatGPT prompt: scared Zippy holding the crown (sprite sheet)

One sprite sheet of Zippy holding the crown while he is scared, for the moment after a wrong swap: the balance tips, the alarm rings and Grumble wakes up. Today the game shows his scared poses with the crown floating in front of him. With this sheet his hands really hold it.

## How to use it

1. Open ChatGPT and start a new chat (image generation must be available).
2. Attach these three images from this folder:
   - `zippy swap.png`, so ChatGPT draws the same Zippy, hanging the same way;
   - `fail monkey.png`, for his scared faces;
   - `crown.png`, so the crown in his hands is exactly the game's crown.
3. Paste the prompt below and send it.
4. Download the image and save it in this folder as `zippy scared crown.png`, then tell Claude.

## The prompt (copy everything in the box)

```
Create ONE sprite sheet for a cartoon children's math game: Zippy the monkey is scared while he holds a golden crown. He has just made a mistake and a sleepy monster is waking up, so he is nervous and holds the crown tight. It is funny and cute, never really frightening.

Zippy must be EXACTLY the same character as in the attached sprite sheet zippy swap.png: the same face, orange-brown fur, cream face, hands and tummy, big round ears, curly tail and proportions, with the same bold dark-brown outline and soft cel shading with glossy highlights. Do not redesign him. His scared faces should look like the ones in the attached fail monkey.png.

The crown must be EXACTLY the attached crown (crown.png): shiny gold, five points each topped with a gold ball, three red oval gems on the front (the middle gem is the biggest) and a gold band along the bottom. Same shape, colours and gems in every frame. Do not add or change anything on it.

Canvas and layout:
- Wide landscape image, 1536 × 1024, with a fully transparent background. If transparency is not possible, use one flat, solid, pure magenta (#FF00FF) background with no gradient or texture.
- 8 frames in a 4 × 2 grid: 4 equal columns and 2 equal rows. One Zippy per frame, centred in his cell, with at least 40 px of empty space around him. Nothing touches or crosses into the next cell.
- In every frame Zippy hangs by his tail exactly like in zippy swap.png: his tail rises from his back and curls at the top centre of the cell, at the same height in every frame (do NOT draw the vine). His body hangs upright below it, head up.
- Zippy is the same size in all 8 frames, and the same size as in zippy swap.png: his head is the same size in every frame.
- In every frame he holds the crown with his own hands, his fingers wrapped around its band or its points, so it is clearly held and not floating. The crown is the same size in every frame, about as wide as his head. It never covers his eyes, except in frame 5 where only his eyes peek over it.
- He faces the viewer, turned slightly to the viewer's right.
- No text, numbers, labels, frame borders, grid lines, motion lines, shake marks, sparkles, stars or shadows. No vine, no balance, no monster, no background. No other objects.

The 8 frames, left to right, top row first:
1. Uh-oh: he holds the crown in front of his chest with both hands; eyes wide, mouth open in surprise.
2. Gritted teeth: he hugs the crown tight against his chest, teeth clenched, eyebrows up, worried.
3. Side glance: he holds the crown close and glances sideways towards the viewer's left, nervous.
4. Trembling: he clutches the crown with both hands, knees pulled up, one small blue sweat drop on his forehead.
5. Hiding: he holds the crown up in front of his face, and only his scared eyes peek over its points.
6. Gulp: the crown clutched at his chest, eyes looking up, a tiny worried mouth.
7. Eek!: a jolt of fright, both arms up holding the crown above his head, eyes squeezed shut, mouth wide open.
8. Sheepish: he holds the crown out in front of him with both hands, as if offering it back, with an embarrassed, nervous smile.

Before you finish, check: 8 separate frames in a 4 × 2 grid; the same Zippy at the same size in every frame; the tail curl at the same height in every frame; his hands really hold the crown in every frame; the crown looks exactly like crown.png and is the same size in every frame; the background is transparent; there is no text and nothing else.
```

## If something goes wrong (follow-up messages for the same chat)

- **The crown floats, or his hands don't hold it:**
  > In every frame, make his fingers wrap around the crown's band or points, so he is clearly holding it. Keep everything else the same.
- **The crown looks different from crown.png:**
  > Make the crown exactly like the attached crown.png: five gold points with gold balls, three red oval gems with the biggest in the middle, and a gold band at the bottom. Keep everything else the same.
- **The crown changes size between frames:**
  > Draw the crown at the same size in all 8 frames, about as wide as his head. Keep the poses.
- **He looks too frightened or sad:**
  > Make his scared faces funny and cute, like a cartoon "uh-oh", as in fail monkey.png. Keep the poses and the crown.
- **Zippy hangs upside down, or looks different from the attached sheet:**
  > Make him hang by his tail exactly like in zippy swap.png (tail curling at the top, body upright, head up), and look exactly like him. Keep the poses and the crown.
- **The background is not transparent:**
  > Make the background fully transparent, or one flat pure magenta (#FF00FF). Keep everything else the same.
- **Zippy changes size between frames, or his tail moves up and down:**
  > Draw Zippy at the same size in all 8 frames, with his tail curl at the same height in every frame. Keep the poses.
- **Frames touch, or there are shake marks, sparkles or labels:**
  > Keep exactly 8 frames in a 4 × 2 grid with empty space between them. Remove all motion lines, shake marks, sparkles, text and anything else.

## What happens next

Claude cuts the sheet into 8 frames and lines them up on the tip of Zippy's tail, like his other sheets. Then, after a wrong swap:
- he gets a fright with the crown in his hands as the balance tips (frames 1–2);
- he clutches it nervously while the alarm rings (frames 3–6);
- he jumps when Grumble roars (frame 7), and the crown jumps out of his hands back onto the pan.
