# ChatGPT prompt: Zippy wears the crown (sprite sheet)

One sprite sheet of Zippy putting the crown on his own head after a correct swap, then celebrating while he wears it.

## How to use it

1. Open ChatGPT and start a new chat (image generation must be available).
2. Attach these two images from this folder:
   - `zippy swap.png`, so ChatGPT draws the same Zippy, hanging the same way;
   - `crown.png`, so the crown on his head is exactly the game's crown.

   Don't attach `hold crown.png` or `winning monkey.png`: they show him upside down or holding the crown, and ChatGPT may copy those poses.
3. Paste the prompt below and send it.
4. Download the image and save it in this folder as `zippy crown.png`, then tell Claude.

## The prompt (copy everything in the box)

```
Create ONE sprite sheet for a cartoon children's math game: Zippy the monkey puts a golden crown on his own head and celebrates while wearing it.

Zippy must be EXACTLY the same character as in the attached sprite sheet (zippy swap.png): the same face, orange-brown fur, cream face, hands and tummy, big round ears, curly tail and proportions, with the same bold dark-brown outline and soft cel shading with glossy highlights. Do not redesign him.

The crown must be EXACTLY the attached crown (crown.png): shiny gold, five points each topped with a gold ball, three red oval gems on the front (the middle gem is the biggest) and a gold band along the bottom. Same shape, colours and gems in every frame. Do not add or change anything on it.

Canvas and layout:
- Wide landscape image, 1536 × 1024, with a fully transparent background. If transparency is not possible, use one flat, solid, pure magenta (#FF00FF) background with no gradient or texture.
- 8 frames in a 4 × 2 grid: 4 equal columns and 2 equal rows. One Zippy per frame, centred in his cell, with at least 40 px of empty space around him. Nothing touches or crosses into the next cell.
- In every frame Zippy hangs by his tail exactly like in zippy swap.png: his tail rises from his back and curls at the top centre of the cell, at the same height in every frame (do NOT draw the vine). His body hangs upright below it, head up.
- Zippy is the same size in all 8 frames, and the same size as in zippy swap.png: his head is the same size in every frame.
- The crown is the same size in every frame: about as wide as his head. When it is on his head, it sits on top between his ears, in front of his tail, and never covers his eyes.
- He faces the viewer, turned slightly to the viewer's right.
- No text, numbers, labels, frame borders, grid lines, motion lines, sparkles, stars or shadows. No vine, no balance, no background. No other objects.

The 8 frames, left to right, top row first:
1. Lift: he holds the crown up high above his head with both hands, looking up at it, excited.
2. Put on: he lowers the crown onto his head with both hands on its sides, eyes looking up at it, happy.
3. Wow: the crown is on his head; both hands touch his cheeks, eyes wide, mouth open in wonder.
4. Proud: the crown on his head, chin up, fists on his hips, a big proud smile.
5. Thumbs up: the crown on his head, a thumbs-up with one hand, winking with a big grin.
6. Wave: the crown on his head, waving one open hand at the viewer, big happy smile.
7. Laugh: the crown on his head, laughing with his eyes closed and both hands on his tummy.
8. Adjust: the crown tilted a little to one side on his head; one hand straightens it, with a cheeky grin.

Before you finish, check: 8 separate frames in a 4 × 2 grid; the same Zippy at the same size in every frame; the tail curl at the same height in every frame; the crown looks exactly like crown.png and is the same size in every frame; in frames 3 to 8 the crown sits on top of his head without covering his eyes; the background is transparent; there is no text and nothing else.
```

## If something goes wrong (follow-up messages for the same chat)

- **The crown looks different from crown.png:**
  > Make the crown exactly like the attached crown.png: five gold points with gold balls, three red oval gems with the biggest in the middle, and a gold band at the bottom. Keep everything else the same.
- **The crown covers his eyes, or floats above his head:**
  > In frames 3 to 8, put the crown right on top of his head, between his ears, with his eyes fully visible. Keep everything else the same.
- **The crown changes size between frames:**
  > Draw the crown at the same size in all 8 frames, about as wide as his head. Keep the poses.
- **Zippy hangs upside down, or his head is not upright:**
  > Make him hang by his tail exactly like in zippy swap.png: tail curling at the top, his body upright below it, head up. Keep the poses and the crown.
- **Zippy looks different from the attached sheet:**
  > Make Zippy look exactly like zippy swap.png (same face, ears, fur colour and outline). Keep everything else the same.
- **The background is not transparent:**
  > Make the background fully transparent, or one flat pure magenta (#FF00FF). Keep everything else the same.
- **Zippy changes size between frames, or his tail moves up and down:**
  > Draw Zippy at the same size in all 8 frames, with his tail curl at the same height in every frame. Keep the poses.
- **Frames touch, or there are sparkles, stars or labels:**
  > Keep exactly 8 frames in a 4 × 2 grid with empty space between them. Remove all sparkles, stars, text and anything else.

## What happens next

Claude cuts the sheet into 8 frames and lines them up on the tip of Zippy's tail, like his other sheets, so he swaps from the swap frames to these without a jump. After a correct swap, Zippy then:
- lifts the crown and puts it on his head (frames 1–2);
- says "wow" and stands proud (frames 3–4);
- celebrates while wearing it (frames 5–8), while the game adds the sparkles and confetti.
